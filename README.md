# On the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-Functions

Robert Martinez (REM2) · Preprint · November 26, 2026

[Download the full paper (PDF, 655 pages)](GRHDLF.pdf)

The manuscript presents a proof in prose of the generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. It develops a one-sided criterion from oscillation of smoothed prime sums and gives the arithmetic argument for the twisted estimate. The manuscript credits Levent Alpöge's original formal development and the shorter formalization released by Luke Sernau and collaborators.
